⚛️ Anatomy of an Atom

Every atom consists of positive, negative, and neutral subatomic particles. Click the cards below to toggle properties, verify charges, and solidify active recall definitions.

🔴

Proton

Positive (+1)

Charge: Positive ($+1$)

Location: Inside the nucleus

Identity role: Establishes the Atomic Number

Mass contribution: Heavy (Part of the mass number)

⚪

Neutron

Neutral (0)

Charge: Uncharged / Neutral ($0$)

Location: Inside the nucleus

Role: Stabilizes nuclear structure

Mass contribution: Heavy (Part of the mass number)

🔵

Electron

Negative (-1)

Charge: Negative ($-1$)

Location: Outside nucleus (shells/cloud)

Role: Dictates electrical interactions

Mass contribution: Negligible (considered zero)

💡
Key Concept: Concentrated Mass

Almost 100% of an atom's physical weight is packed directly into the central nucleus because protons and neutrons are dramatically heavier than electrons. The rest of the atom is mostly empty space!
