💡
Key Concept: Concentrated Mass

Almost 100% of an atom's physical weight is packed directly into the central nucleus because protons and neutrons are dramatically heavier than electrons. The rest of the atom is mostly empty space!

4. Food Colouring Demonstration

Why does food coloring spread?
The food colouring spreads because water molecules are in constant motion. And because moving water molecules collide with the food colouring particles, bumping them around and spreading them throughout the container faster.

14. Ice, Water, and Steam States

Ice (Solid)

Particles are tightly packed together and vibrating in fixed positions.

Water (Liquid)

Particles are close together but can slide and tumble past one another.

Steam (Gas)

Particles are far apart and moving rapidly in all directions.

15. How Ice Melts When Heated (KMT)

When ice is heated, the water particles absorb thermal energy and vibrate faster. Eventually, they gain enough kinetic energy to break free from their fixed positions, allowing them to slide past one another and change into a liquid.

16. Water Molecule Challenge Questions
Q1. What elements make up water?

Water is made up of hydrogen and oxygen.

Q2. If a proton were added to an oxygen atom, what would happen?

The atomic number would change from 8 to 9, transforming the oxygen atom into a completely different element called Fluorine.
